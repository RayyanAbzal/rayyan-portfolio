# Surface brief: index.html (Home), with work.html and about.html

Scope: replacement visual world for the whole site (Home, Projects, About, four case pages, 404). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: non-technical founders and operators deciding whether to book a free intro call. Action: book on Calendly. Proof: four client case studies with synthetic-data schematics, three role-attributed testimonials, the fixed-price 48h process. Constraints: no client screenshots, no photo of Rayyan, keep SEO head and JSON-LD, keep case URLs, plain static HTML.

Chosen direction: the calm image-led structure of bencium.io (user-pinned reference, 2026-09-04) in Rayyan's own materials. Memorable moment: the hero panel, one giant tight headline on a soft sage field with a single dark pill button, then the four product schematics as the first thing below the fold.

Unresolved: client names on testimonials (pending approval); Rayyan portrait (none supplied).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet room with one loud sentence. Refuses the "terminal dashboard" arrangement (mono labels, grid texture, status pills, at-a-glance tables) the old site used and most AI-consultant sites default to.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white warm ground `#f5f3ee`, white surfaces `#ffffff` on a 1px hairline at 24px radius, a sage-grey well `#e6ebe3` behind each card schematic, pine-dark `#1f3d2b` for every pill button and one soft sage field `#dfe8dc` for the hero panel, kowhai gold `#f3c94a` as the single marker highlight. Inter Tight everywhere, tight tracking on display, no monospace at all; schematic data is the same face with tabular numerals.

STORY: "He builds the thing himself, it ships, here is what it did" then book.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim nav (name, three links, dark pill "Book a call"). Below, a full-width rounded sage panel holding a 6-7rem two-line headline top-left, a 60ch sub, one dark pill and one text link. The panel ends before the fold with the first two project cards peeking.

FORM: Brief-pinned reference world (bencium.io), no roll, seed key: none (user-pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
