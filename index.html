Three ways in.

Most engagements start with finding where AI or automation can replace manual work. When the project needs a whole product, I build that too.

A manual process is eating your week.

I find the bottleneck, then build the automation or AI workflow that removes it. Your team keeps the tools they know while the busywork disappears. Usually one to three weeks.

I add LLM features, agents, and pipelines to the codebase you already have. Most engagements are additive rather than a rebuild, so your team keeps shipping while I work. Usually two to four weeks.
